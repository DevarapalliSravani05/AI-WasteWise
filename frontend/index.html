ABOUT AI WASTEWISE

Technology for a Cleaner Tomorrow

AI WasteWise is an AI-powered sustainability concept designed to help people make better waste-management decisions.

By providing simple and understandable recommendations, the system aims to encourage responsible consumption and waste segregation.

12

SDG 12

Responsible Consumption and Production

RESPONSIBLE AI

AI You Can Trust

AI WasteWise is designed to provide useful guidance while recognizing uncertainty and protecting users.

⚖️

Fairness

Avoid biased assumptions and aim to provide consistent waste-classification guidance.

🔍

Transparency

The system displays an AI confidence level and avoids presenting uncertain results as facts.

🛡️

Privacy

The prototype does not require personal information to classify waste.

🌱

Ethical Use

AI recommendations are intended to support responsible waste decisions, not replace official local waste-management guidance.
